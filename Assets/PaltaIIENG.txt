<h1>Basso Palta II<br /><small>The next frontier</small></h1>
<p>The proof that an adventure can be competitive, that performance doesn&rsquo;t require a race number, that exploration is better with adrenaline and speed&hellip;Comes in the form of Palta.</p>
<h3>Others observe, we experience: Others do gravel, we do Palta</h3>
<p>While others tried to define gravel, we marked our own path.<br />Others tried to follow a definition that didn&rsquo;t exist, we created one coherent with our DNA.</p>
<hr />
<h2>Geometry</h2>
<p>Minor changes were made to the extremely versatile Palta geometry.</p>
<h3>Sloping</h3>
<p>Incresed sloping value to allow more seatpost exposure and flex leading to increased comfort.</p>
<h3>Headtube</h3>
<p>Taller headtube for less spacers and more comfort. But we didn&rsquo;t forget about the racers&hellip; Riders will have the possibility to mount the stem flush with the headtube for lower stack.</p>
<hr />
<h2>Design</h2>
<p>The design of the Palta II&nbsp;follows the philosophy of maintaining the overwhelmingly long list of positive elements from the first version and improving, where possible.</p>
<h3>Fork</h3>
<p>The unique geometry of the advanced fork stays confer both a more precise handling as well as vibration dampening to the ride quality.</p>
<h3>Air Flow</h3>
<p>Larger spacing between tire and fork allows for better air flow and increased clearance.</p>
<hr />
<h2>Frame features</h2>
<h3>Tire clearance</h3>
<p>Tire clearance up to 45 mm tires with 700c wheels. NOTE: Indicated tire clearance is guaranteed with gravel groupsets. Road bike groupsets follow manufacturer suggested clearance.</p>
<h3>Bags &amp; Bottles</h3>
<p>Top tube hidden bolts to accommodate compatible bags. 3 different bottle cage positions: 2 on the downtube and 1 on the seat tube.</p>
<h3>Protection &amp; Compatibility</h3>
<p>The downtube also features a plastic protection that can be fitted when riding on rough gravel. Palta is compatible with both 2x and 1x electronic and mechanical groupsets, both gravel and road (up to 52-36 chain rings).</p>
<hr />
<h2>3B Clamp System Gen 2</h2>
<p>A totally hidden fixing solution that is invisible on the frame, intertwining technological and aesthetic innovation.</p>
<p>The result is a lightweight, invisible and strong integrated system with three screws, which has been further implemented through the vulcanization of the steel plank. This Basso solution gives the 3B system an anti-vibration function, which improves the riding experience by elevating comfort on uneven and bumpy surfaces.</p>
<h2>Basso Explorer Thru Axle Kit</h2>
<p>The Basso Explorer thru axle kit is composed by a front and rear axle with specific integrated cycling tools. In this way you will always carry with you the necessary tools to keep on riding in every situation.</p>
<hr />
<h2>The cockpit</h2>
<h3>Compatibility</h3>
<p>The Paradigma Integration System was born from the desire to create a solution for the complete integration of cables compatible with all types of disc brake group sets, 1x and 2x, electronic and mechanical.</p>
<h3>Handling</h3>
<p>The passage of many cables in a small space often results in a slowdown and heavy steering. Basso, through this system, is able to integrate mechanical cable groups with front derailleur, maintaining maximum steering lightness to confer precise handling.</p>
<h3>Practicality</h3>
<p>The Paradigma stem remains free from cables so that it can be removed at any time without the need to disconnect the cables. This makes it easier to maintain the bike, transport it in travel bags and customize the length of the stem.</p>
